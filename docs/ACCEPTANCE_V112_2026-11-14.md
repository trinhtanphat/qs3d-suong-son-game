## PHA hardware read-only recovery instrumentation

The remaining **issue #2** is blocked by DESKTOP-PHA1S90 being offline, not by QS3D application code. A new **read-only diagnostic collector** `pha-whea-readonly-collector.ps1` and field checklist `PHA_READONLY_RECOVERY_RUNBOOK_2026-11-14.md` have been committed to the audit records. The collector was syntax-checked and safely trialed on **WIN11-181 only**; that successful trial is **not** evidence that PHA hardware is healthy.

The collector captures WHEA 17 counts, last 24-hour WHEA presence, unexpected shutdowns, uptime, BIOS/model, relevant PCIe/WLAN PnP devices and Wi-Fi driver versions without changing devices, drivers, BIOS, network or production. Hardware repair requires the PHA device to reconnect and may require an on-site technician with fallback connectivity. See [issue #2](https://github.com/trinhtanphat/qs3d-suong-son-game/issues/2).
